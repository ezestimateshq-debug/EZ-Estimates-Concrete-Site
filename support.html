What to gather before setup

  • Company name, phone, email, and address
  • Proposal header preference
  • Standard proposal notes, terms, and exclusions
  • Local concrete price assumptions
  • Rebar price assumptions if applicable
  • Labor rates or crew assumptions
  • Equipment, pump, cleanup, demo, haul-off, disposal, and cure/seal assumptions
  • Default markup
  • One real job for the first estimate

First estimate checklist

  • Create or select customer and project.
  • Add slab, footing, pier, paving, sidewalk, curb, or other concrete work item.
  • Enter dimensions or quantity.
  • Add supported rebar workflow if relevant.
  • Add pump, cleanup, demo, haul-off, disposal, equipment, or cure and seal if relevant.
  • Review direct cost, markup, margin, tax, and total bid.
  • Generate internal report and customer proposal.
  • Save, reopen, and edit the estimate.

Common beginner questions

Does the app decide my pricing?

No. You enter or approve your own local pricing assumptions.

Should I send the internal report to the customer?

No. The internal report is for cost and margin review. Use the customer proposal for customer-facing scope and pricing.

Does the app do takeoff?

EZ Estimates Concrete is focused on estimating workflow, costs, margin, and proposals. Use your takeoff tool, field measurements, or known job dimensions for quantities.

What if my price assumptions change?

Update your pricing assumptions for new work. Saved estimate rows keep copied values so old bids do not change behind your back.

Request help

Send your company name, contact name, app version, what you were trying to do, what happened, and whether the issue blocks a bid.

Do not send private customer pricing, customer details, or bid totals unless needed for support and approved by the contractor.
