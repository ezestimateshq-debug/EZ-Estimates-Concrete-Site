FAQ

Does EZ Estimates Concrete do takeoff?

EZ Estimates Concrete is focused on estimating workflow, costs, margin review, and proposals. Use your takeoff tool, field measurements, or known job dimensions for quantities, then build and review the bid in EZ Estimates Concrete.

Is this a CRM or construction management system?

No. EZ Estimates Concrete is not trying to run your whole company. It is focused on concrete estimating, internal cost review, saved estimates, exports, and customer proposals.

Does it know my local prices?

No software should guess your local prices. EZ Estimates Concrete gives you the structure to enter and reuse your own concrete, rebar, labor, equipment, markup, tax, and other assumptions.

Does rebar work automatically?

No. Rebar workflows are estimating tools where supported. The contractor decides what reinforcement is required and what local price applies.
